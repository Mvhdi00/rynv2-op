The moment a song is added — or played for the first time — LRC AI finds its synced lyrics online, translates them to English if they are not, and hands them to chat sync. No searching. Each song is looked up once and kept.
The button on every song shows where it is: ✓ LRC is ready — click it for details and a per-song offset. Retry LRC means nothing matched; click it to search again.
Add song▼
How to add a song with its lyrics
  1. Find the song on youtube.com and copy its link.
  2. Turn the link into a file on ytmp3.gl and download the .mp3.
  3. Search the same song on lrclib.net and download the synced lyrics as .lrc.
  4. Fill in the Title below and pick the .mp3 in the file box, or paste a direct link in URL.
  5. In the LRC sync box, pick the .lrc file — or paste its lines into the text area.
  6. Press Add song. It shows up in the library with its lyrics attached.
With LRC AI on, steps 3 and 5 are optional: add the song with just its title and file, and the lyrics are found for you. A file whose tags or title carry the real song name and artist is found most reliably.
The .lrc has to be the synced kind — the one whose lines start with a timestamp like [01:23.45]. Plain lyrics still show up, but they will not follow the song. If the words drift, an .lrc from a different release of the track is usually the reason.
